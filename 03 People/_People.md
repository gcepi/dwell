The six values are `internal` for colleagues, `vendor` for contractors and suppliers, `broker`, `owner` for owners and investors, `resident`, and `other`.

## Rules

An agent creates a contact only on an explicit instruction: add this person, update them, set or clear attention. A name in an email thread or a calendar invite is not permission.

For someone Graham only wants to be able to find again, one line in [[03 People/Names to remember|Names to remember]] is lighter and easier to undo.

No dossiers. No inferred sentiment. No judgment about a colleague's competence, motives, or character. No resident financial, medical, or identity detail, ever. He works with these people, and a note in a git repository outlives the mood that produced it.

The 50-conversations campaign lives in gOS, along with `conversation_date` and `conversation_scheduled`. Those properties do not exist here.
