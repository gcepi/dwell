# System

## What exists now

- **Current structure marker:** `01 Home/` · `02 Notes/` · `03 System/`. There should be no `07 System/` folder in this vault.
- [[01 Home/Dashboard|Dashboard]]: today’s work, decisions, portfolio, and system output.
- [[01 Home/Hessel|Hessel]]: working questions and loose ends for Hessel conversations.
- [[01 Home/Open loops|Open loops]]: a small, human-maintained list of unresolved questions and follow-through. Asana owns tasks.
- [[01 Home/Notes|Notes]]: running observations in your own words.
- `02 Notes/`: every substantive work note, surfaced by its frontmatter type.
- `03 System/`: templates, scripts, sources, logs, and documentation.

## What it does

It preserves source material, makes it findable, gives you action-oriented views, and prepares a handoff to your next workday.

## What it does not do

- It does not replace AppFolio, Google Drive, Gmail, Calendar, or Asana.
- It does not make company commitments, send messages, or move money on its own.
- It does not treat a transcript or handwritten page as more certain than it is.
- It does not automatically share work material with gOS.
- It does not manage committed execution work. Asana will own that layer.

## Use it

1. Start at [[01 Home/Dashboard|Dashboard]].
2. Keep [[01 Home/Hessel|Hessel]] open around conversations with Hessel.
3. Write observations in [[01 Home/Notes|Notes]] and preserve original captures.
4. Use [[01 Home/Review|Review]] once a week.

## Current constraints

- Grain’s credential is stored securely. The first read-only validation is pending a gateway secret-injection repair.
- Supernote material is sourced from the personal Google Drive Export folder, then preserved before interpretation.
