## Review by hand

Four questions, once a week. None of these can be automated, because each one is a judgment.

1. **SOP drafts.** Which drafts in [[05 Work OS/SOPs/|SOPs]] are right enough to set `active`? Which are wrong and should be revised before anyone follows them?
2. **SOPs past `review_due`.** Is the procedure still what the company does?
3. **Projects with no recent update.** Open [[05 Work OS/Projects/|Projects]] and check the `## Updates` section of each active one. A project with nothing dated in 3 weeks is either finished, stalled, or not real.
4. **Properties with no recent log line.** Open [[05 Work OS/Properties/|Properties]]. Silence on an asset in `renovating` or `stabilizing` usually means the record is behind reality.
