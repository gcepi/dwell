# Hessel recap contract

Use [[03 System/Sources/Hessel weekly recap 2026-10-02|Graham's Oct. 2 recap]] as the authoritative style example.

## Shape

1. `Hess,`
2. `Here's the recap for this week.`
3. `High level projects that we are pushing forward:`
   - Numbered projects
   - Short factual sub-bullets
4. `Outstanding items (with action steps):`
   - Named item
   - Parenthetical or nested next step with real timing when known
5. `Key takeaways:`
   - Graham's interpretation, learning, concerns, and management observations

## Content standard

- Use Grain, Supernote, Dwell notes, and calendar evidence from the actual week.
- Preserve uncertainty Graham expressed. Do not manufacture confidence or resolve an open question.
- Include operational detail when it explains the work: people, properties, dates, systems, launch timing, and concrete next steps.
- Write in Graham's plain first-person voice. The recap may include reflection when it is grounded in his source material.
- Keep personal material outside Dwell out of the recap.
